### C2. Every canonical URL points to a domain that does not resolve — and appears to be owned by a third party
- `dig +short A workforce360.dimension.com` → **empty** (no DNS record).
- `dimension.com` → `52.20.84.62`, nameservers `ns1/ns2.atom.com`, WHOIS shows **"Make offer"** / marketplace parking, registrant in UAE. This is a parked, for-sale domain owned by someone else.
- The brand is spelled **DIMENSHION** (`navigation.json:4`, `about/page.tsx:8`, `layout.tsx:25`, `README.md:139`) — there are **zero** references to `dimenshion.com`, and `dimenshion.com` itself does not resolve either.

This string `https://workforce360.dimension.com` is baked into: `src/app/layout.tsx:7` (metadataBase), `public/robots.txt:8`, all 85 sitemap `<loc>`s, `llms.txt:10`, `llms-full.txt:95`, canonical tags in 27 pages, all breadcrumbs, and every JSON-LD block.

**Impact:** Nothing is indexable/canonical at a live domain; social cards, structured data, and the sitemap all advertise a dead third-party URL; risk of trademark/confusion issues on someone else's domain.

**Action:** Confirm the real domain you own (e.g. `dimenshion.com` subdomain or a `.in`/`.io` brand domain), point DNS at the deployment, then do a single global find/replace of the host across `src/`, `public/`, `content/`.

### C3. The demo & contact forms are fake — they promise a callback and send nothing
- `src/components/DemoForm.tsx:30-33` — `handleSubmit` only calls `e.preventDefault(); setSubmitted(true)`. **No POST, no endpoint, no CRM, no email.**
- Success screen (`DemoForm.tsx:42-49`) tells the visitor: *"A Workforce360 enterprise solutions architect … will contact you at {email} within 4 business hours."* Reinforced on `/contact` ("4-Hour Enterprise Turnaround").
- `src/app/contact/page.tsx:41-59` — **no email address, no phone number, no form**. "Corporate Headquarters" shows no address. Grep for `mailto:`/`tel:` across the repo: **zero matches**.

Every lead generated by the site is silently lost, and the promise made to the visitor is false.

**Action:** Wire the forms to a real endpoint (Resend/Postmark/Formspree/HubSpot/your API route), add a real `mailto:` + phone + registered address, and put a `POST` handler behind it.
