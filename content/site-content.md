## 1. The Core Paradigm Shift
Traditional HR applications were built as passive databases to record employee records, log PTO, and generate basic salary slips for desk workers.

Workforce360 is built as an active **Workforce Operating System (WOS)** that connects:
1. **PEOPLE:** Core HRIS, Employee Lifecycle, AI ATS, Performance & OKRs, Learning LMS, Employee Experience.
2. **WORK:** Workforce Management, Biometric Attendance Hardware Sync, 24/7 Shift Rostering, Geofenced Field Operations.
3. **MONEY:** Enterprise Payroll, Indian Statutory Tax (TDS, PF, ESIC, PT), Expense Reimbursements, General Ledger sync.
4. **OPERATIONS & IT:** IT Asset Tracking, Software License Governance, Offboarding Asset Recovery, 19 Industry Vertical Packs.
5. **INTELLIGENCE:** Embedded AI Digital Brain (40 Genkit flows), Workforce Digital Twin Scenario Simulator, Predictive Telemetry.

## 3. The Three Foundational Enterprise Moats
1. **Workforce Digital Twin:** Model restructuring, downsizing, or expansion scenarios and calculate real-time P&L delta, statutory severance liabilities, and critical skill deficits before changes are committed.
2. **Dual-Workforce Engine:** Single unified data model supporting corporate salaried executives on annual CTC alongside industrial plant workers, maritime crews, and contractual staff on daily rates, piece-rate gang cards (per kg yield), and shift allowances.
3. **19 Pre-Built Industry Vertical Packs:** Purpose-built workflows, roles, shift rules, and compliance templates for Shipping & Maritime, Seafood Processing, Logistics & Freight, Manufacturing, Chemical Operations, Healthcare, and 13 other sectors.
