## 1. The Core Paradigm Shift
Traditional HR applications were built as passive systems to record employee records, log PTO, and generate basic salary slips for desk workers.

SpotSign AI is built as an active **Workforce Operating System (WOS)** that connects:
1. **PEOPLE:** Core HRIS, Employee Lifecycle, Recruitment ATS, Performance & OKRs, Learning LMS, Employee Experience.
2. **WORK:** Workforce Management, Biometric Attendance Hardware Sync, 24/7 Shift Rostering, Geofenced Field Operations.
3. **MONEY:** Enterprise Payroll, Indian Statutory Tax (TDS, PF, ESIC, PT), Expense Reimbursements, General Ledger sync.
4. **OPERATIONS & IT:** IT Asset Tracking, Software License Governance, Offboarding Asset Recovery, 19 Industry Vertical Packs.
5. **INSIGHTS:** Workforce Analytics, Executive Reporting, Workforce Trend Analytics, and a dedicated AI capabilities overview at /ai.
